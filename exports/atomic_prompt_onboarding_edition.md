### The reader cohort offer

Your reader code carries a **book-cohort discount** on your first Architect month. The natural moment to use it is **Badge 4 (Floor Manager)** — the first time you run the full FORGE line and earn a certification tier. That is the book's Chapter 3 made real, and it is exactly where Explorer readers convert to Architect.

### The cost guard rail (so finance can say yes)

Every engine run has a monthly cost ceiling per tier. When you hit it, the platform returns a hard stop (`COST_CAP_EXCEEDED`) rather than billing into the unknown. There is no surprise overage — a deliberate design choice so that a team can authorise a broad rollout without runaway-spend risk. (There is no soft warning before the cap; plan your heavy F8 days accordingly.)

## BADGE 0 — "KNOW YOUR NUMBER"
**Difficulty: ▲ (trivial) · Anchored to: Prologue + "The Three Numbers" · Tier: Explorer (free)**

- **The story beat.** The prologue's whole terror is one figure: David's JST of 67.4, "high vulnerability, eighteen months from obsolescence." Before you can change your number, you have to know it.
- **Do this (≈ 5 min).** On first sign-in, complete the **JST self-assessment** (Jobs · Skills · Talent). The platform stores your baseline composite JST.
- **Unlock condition.** A JST baseline is recorded to your profile.
- **What you walk away with.** Your personal JST score and vulnerability band — the same instrument the book's transformation is measured against.
- **Unlocks →** Badge 1.

## BADGE 1 — "MONSTER HUNTER"
**Difficulty: ▲ (trivial) · Anchored to: Chapter 1, "The Monster We Built" · Tier: Explorer**

- **The story beat.** Chapter 1 names the monster — the 6.47× displacement gap. Hunting it starts with *seeing* prompt quality objectively for the first time.
- **Do this (≈ 5 min).** Fork one exemplar from the **BONSAI library** and run **F1** (the diagnostic) on it. Read the JCSE score it returns.
- **Unlock condition.** One completed F1 run with a JCSE score displayed.
- **What you walk away with.** Your first JCSE-scored diagnostic — proof you can now measure prompt quality, not guess at it.
- **Unlocks →** Badge 2.

## BADGE 2 — "ATOM SMITH"
**Difficulty: ▲▲ (easy) · Anchored to: Chapter 2, "The Specification" · Tier: Explorer**

- **The story beat.** Chapter 2 is the 7-tuple `(I, O, C, T, M, V, Δ)` — the moment a prompt stops being "ad-hoc text" and becomes an engineered artifact. The SSN bug failed because it had no Input Contract; you are about to write one.
- **Do this (≈ 10 min).** Run **F2** on a problem you actually own, producing your first **Atomic Prompt** with all seven tuple components. Use the free **book Cartridge** (ACME worked examples) so you start warm.
- **Unlock condition.** One F2 output with all 7 components present.
- **What you walk away with.** Your first production-grade Atomic Prompt — testable, versionable, reusable.
- **Unlocks →** Badge 3.

## BADGE 3 — "FLOOR MANAGER" ★ conversion rung
**Difficulty: ▲▲▲ (moderate) · Anchored to: Chapter 3, "The Production Floor" · Tier: Architect ($199/mo — use your reader discount here)**

- **The story beat.** Chapter 3 builds the factory: FORGE's seven phases, quality gates, the BONSAI cultivation layer. This is where craft becomes manufacturing — and where you stop being a tool user and become a line operator.
- **Do this (≈ 20 min).** Walk **F3 → F4 → F5**: grow the CELL micro-agent, convert to a Micro PDD, then produce a full 15-section SPC. Earn your **first certification tier** (SILVER or above).
- **Unlock condition.** One F5 SPC with a JCSE total ≥ 35/50 (Near-Production or better) and a cert tier assigned.
- **What you walk away with.** Your first certified specification — the artifact that proves you can run the whole production line, not just one station. **This is the moment to convert to Architect.**
- **Unlocks →** Badge 4.

## BADGE 4 — "PILLAR ARCHITECT"
**Difficulty: ▲▲▲ (moderate) · Anchored to: Chapter 4 = *Context Craft* (companion) · Tier: Architect**

- **The story beat.** Chapter 4 of the Atomic Prompt curriculum *is* Context Craft — the seven pillars that decide what goes *inside* each tuple component. They are JCSE's entire "Context Engineering" dimension (20 of the 50 points).
- **Do this (≈ 20 min).** Work the seven pillars one at a time (SYSTEM, ROLE, INSTRUCTION, DATA, CONSTRAINT, FORMAT, EXAMPLE), re-running F1 after each, until your **Context Engineering sub-score climbs to ≥ 16/20**. (Open the *Context Craft* companion book for the deep dive on any pillar that lags.)
- **Unlock condition.** A diagnostic with Context Engineering ≥ 16/20.
- **What you walk away with.** Mastery of the input-quality layer — the difference between a prompt that works and one that survives an adversary.
- **Unlocks →** Badge 5.
