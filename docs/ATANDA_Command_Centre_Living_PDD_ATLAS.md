## PART 2 · Executive Summary

*Audience: C-Suite, product owners, sponsors · Format: prose + tables*

### 1. Business Context (The WHY)

The market gap the Command Centre addresses is **reproducible AI methodology**. Teams have access to LLMs and prompt tools, but none produce a single, certified, portable blueprint that converts a raw idea into a verifiable deliverable in one coherent session. The Command Centre closes that gap by productising the FORGE.BONSAI HARNESS — an instruction layer of ordered atomic prompts that drives a deterministic prompt → SPC → PDD → certified MVP-PDD pipeline, and then scaffolds and plans the hosting for the result.

The deployed system is **DEPLOYED at MVP**: the portal shell, F1–F7 workspaces, F8 Code DJ, the F8-HDJ HOST DJ engine, billing, badges, orgs, ingestion, cartridge, and the public verify surface are all live. Higher-cost surfaces are gated by subscriber tier and a monthly cost cap rather than feature flags, so commercial scope lifts without a redeploy.
