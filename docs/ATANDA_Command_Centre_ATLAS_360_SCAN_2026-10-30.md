## PART 1 — CHEAT SHEET

### Executive finding

ACC has an implemented governed specification-production floor and a broad set of supporting
tools. Its operational claims must remain narrower than its code footprint: protected
publication workflows, independent verification and full deployment operation have unresolved
or conditional prerequisites.

The most recent customer-facing addition is AutoCommand. Its public product catalog,
individually verified customer access, private saved intake and source uploads are development
verified. Automatic specification generation, commercial settlement and final IMPRIMA email
fulfillment are not yet connected.

### What changed since the previous SCAN

- AutoCommand now has a customer storefront inside ACC at `/autocommand`.
- Its three product prices and future inactive product choices are explicit.
- Verified customers can save, reopen and privately retain an intake with original source filenames.
- Corporate owner/creator fields and optional logo intake are supported at the draft level.
- Customer logout now returns to the public storefront and removes the private workspace from view.
- A dedicated 26-page AutoCommand ATLAS 360 PLAN records 45 build prompts and launch criteria.
- A 30 October administrator handoff records specific unresolved protected public-schema findings.
- The AutoCommand PLAN explicitly requires reuse of IMPRIMA.PORTAL's existing email system.

### Immediate release position

Keep AutoCommand checkout disabled until all three promised package paths, payment/free-offer
settlement, publication authorization and actual delivery pass acceptance. Demonstrate its
intake-only milestone honestly. Do not publish the paid storefront as a completed service.

This report does not conclude that the already published ACC must be taken offline. It also
does not attest that every existing feature is safe or production accepted.

### SCAN 2 — Baseline and invariants

ATANDA is the corporate identity; ARK is the platform. The existing production floor remains
valid. Simplification means reducing customer effort, not deleting mandatory safeguards.

Key invariants:

- Every newly produced project artifact title starts with its project name.
- Artifact type, exact source/version and stage provenance remain distinct.
- A Boomerang return is reviewed supporting input, not automatic gate acceptance or release.
- Remediation generates a new version and requires a fresh affected evaluation.
- Owner, creator, subscriber and publisher are different roles; corporate ownership is declared,
  not inferred from a display name.
- Customer sources and publication packages remain private by default.
- Missing or independent evidence cannot be represented as PASS.
- GRO, Honesty Gate, ZPOS and SAVANT claims must match what actually ran or was installed.
- No fragmentary target package implies that mandatory governance is enforced at that target.
- F8 output-language policy currently permits Python, JavaScript, SQL, C, C++ and C#; it does
  not add TypeScript merely because the ACC application's own code uses TypeScript.
- The selected extended-model route is managed OpenRouter; model advice requires explicit apply.
- AutoCommand excludes F9–F12 and promises specifications/guidance, not an installed application.

#### F6 and F8 boundary corrections

F6 uses eligible F5 artifacts, including qualifying SCAN outputs, rather than arbitrary intake
or foreign-session material. Keep that source boundary in the automatic adapters.

The older 28 October report described a TypeScript/JavaScript scaffold. That wording must not

#### Support boxes and upgraded-artifact return

Boomerang, SPC Player, F0 advice, IMPRIMA, Agent Creator, Ingestion and Cartridge are intended

what it does, the expected outcome and the route back to the stage they left.

version with retained lineage; repeat remediation increments the upgrade/version label.
Do not guarantee a corrected version passes drift simply because a rework step completed.

Prior source/scoped-browser observations support parts of the stage cockpit and return design.

#### Agent Creator and legacy SHORT SPRINTS

Agent Creator is the replacement subscriber-facing feature. Historical SHORT SPRINTS records
and protected workflows remain relevant; replacement is not deletion of their history or
permission to bypass their safeguards.

The creation control activates only for eligible saved MA/Micro PDD pairs at F3/F4 or a saved
SPC at F5. It is not an always-active shortcut on every stage. An SPC Cheat Sheet is the first
saved creation companion and contains the nine required identity, lineage, timestamp, skill,
SAVANT, constraints, author and owner fields.

Standard-floor Agent Creator may pursue eligible in-ARK and third-party operation. AutoCommand
uses its specification-preparation capability only: no automatic agent launch or F10–F12 promise.

#### IMPRIMA.PORTAL and email

IMPRIMA.PORTAL already has an email-based publication system. Current source includes publication
preparation, emailed publication-code handling, redemption, exact-byte attachments, scoped email
transport and control-copy checks.

Subscriber/enterprise email configuration is part of the existing system. AutoCommand must reuse
it; the missing work is the order/package handoff and acceptance, not building another mail service.

The existence of email helpers does not establish current provider availability or successful
live publication. Failed/dry-run mail cannot be marked SENT. A control-copy failure remains an
operational failure rather than permission to issue an unprotected download.

The launch-readiness text still mentions incomplete IMPRIMA/SHORT SPRINTS gates. Interpret it
as an unresolved governance boundary, not evidence that the newer Portal has no working code.
Operator-facing wording should eventually distinguish those separate scopes.

## PART 4 — IMPLEMENTATION DISPOSITION

### Priority sequence

1. Preserve the working AutoCommand intake and explicit availability notices.
2. Approve the exact package inventory and specification-only stage adapters.
3. Resolve the protected publication prerequisites through the reviewed administrator path.
4. Define the AutoCommand-to-IMPRIMA authorization contract using the existing email system.

7. Complete Basic, Advanced and Web package rendering, attribution, stamps and editable F0 DOCX.

9. Verify the critical free/paid/restart/failure journeys and collect a human GO/HOLD decision.
10. Update atanda.ai navigation in its own source project and publish only after acceptance.

Payment collection remains disabled while fulfillment is incomplete, even if checkout code
is implemented earlier in the dependency sequence.

### Tool and responsibility posture

Use the existing Replit/React/Vite/Express/PostgreSQL infrastructure, private App Storage,
generated contracts, existing engine adapters, Stripe connection and IMPRIMA.PORTAL.
No replacement email system or parallel security architecture is required by this report.

ATLAS structures the analysis; SPARTAN/CODON/CORDON and CODE DJ concepts may guide bounded
scope and quality review. Such doctrine is an analyst overlay unless the actual engine
implementation and evidence prove runtime enforcement.

Assign accountable humans for product/price policy, publication/security authorization,
operations and release. A polished PDF, passing type check or model-generated confidence
does not replace their approval.

### Go-live acceptance

- Three product paths produce all promised artifacts from real saved sources.
- F0 business drafts are editable DOCX with clearly marked assumptions.
- Prices and first-free eligibility are server-authoritative and concurrency-safe.
- Private customer access, source files, orders and packages are isolated.
- Generation survives disconnection without duplicate charges or paid work.
- Failed evaluations remain blocked or follow bounded evidence-based remediation.
- Existing IMPRIMA authorization and email/control-copy behavior is preserved.
- Recipient, author, owner, timestamps, manifest and exact attachment bytes are correct.
- Failure, refund, delayed-payment and mail-retry outcomes are understandable and recoverable.
- The separate atanda.ai handoff is verified.
- A human-approved release record supports the precise advertised offering.
