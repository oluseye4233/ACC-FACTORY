# MATHMON Integration Concept Note
## Strengthening the ARK Harness F1–F9 Production Floor

### Executive Summary
This concept note proposes the integration of MATHMON (Mathematical Verification and Applicability Engine) into the ARK Harness ecosystem as a cross-cutting validation, simulation, optimization, and applicability layer.

The objective is to transform cognitive artifacts from being merely logically coherent into assets that are measurable, simulatable, certifiable, and economically valuable.
