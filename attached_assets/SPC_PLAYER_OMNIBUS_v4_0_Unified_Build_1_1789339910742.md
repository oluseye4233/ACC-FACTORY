Honesty Gate G3 --- On This Consolidation

Three predecessor documents in this chain --- the v2.0 base PDD (-002),
the Open Items addendum (-003), and the Voice Campaigns/TITAN-corrected
addendum (-004 v1.1) --- have never been located as standalone files in
this project across two separate extraction passes. Their content
reaches this v4.0 build only as already filtered through -005, -006, and
-007's own restatements. This build does not claim to have read them
directly, and does not restate anything from them beyond what
-005/-006/-007 already carried forward.

On "ensure it is compliant with all internal quality and compliance
checks": this platform defines specific checks --- SOLVA-LITE, the
Linguistic Detectability Pass, the Universal Clarity Pass, Honesty Gate
G3 itself, JCSE self-scoring, and FORGE certification via independent
SOLVA GAUNTLET adjudication. This build runs every one of those checks
in self-adjudication mode and reports the result honestly in Part 4,
including the checks that do not pass cleanly. It does not report full
compliance where none exists --- doing so would itself violate G3, the
same rule this document is trying to satisfy. "Compliant with every
internal check" and "every internal check has actually been run and
passed independently" are different claims; this build makes only the
first, and states plainly where the second remains open.

Part 2 · Executive Summary

Why This Build

The v3.0 Complete Rewrite (Aug 27--28, 2026) consolidated five amendment
documents into one canonical 14-domain PDD, but it predates two things
that then shipped downstream, inside a single card's document rather
than the platform's own: the SPC Cheat Sheet standard (REVERB ALPHA
v2.0, §7, Aug 30) and the explicit three-axis score-separation rule
(REVERB ALPHA v2.0, C-REV-10, also Aug 30). Both were written as if
generally applicable --- REVERB ALPHA's own text calls the Cheat Sheet
"a new standard feature for every SPC" --- but neither was ever folded
back into the SPC PLAYER Omnibus PDD itself. This build closes that gap:
features scattered across the REVERB card lineage that were always meant
to be platform-wide are now native to the platform document, not
borrowed by reference from a downstream card.

What's Genuinely New

-   Domain 14: SPC Cheat Sheet & Marketplace Discovery Standard ---
    generalizes REVERB ALPHA §7's per-card pattern (General Definition,
    Production Process, ten named Environment Sketches) into a
    platform-wide publication requirement, owned by SPHINX ENGINE
    consistent with its already-established Domain 3/5 marketplace role.

-   Domain 1 (+FOUND-010): the SPC Dev Kit --- ATLAS 360 PLAN, SPARTAN,
    CODE DJ, BUGMXT, CODON, CORDON --- is now a named platform registry
    entry, not a pattern known only from a single card's Synergy Map
    (REVERB ALPHA §5).

-   Domain 13 (+KLARITY-003): the rule that Clarity, Truthfulness, and
    Detectability are always reported as three separate figures ---
    never one composite score --- is now a platform-wide requirement for
    any card's output package, generalized from REVERB ALPHA's
    card-specific C-REV-10.

What Was Considered and Deliberately Not Adopted Platform-Wide

REVERB v3.0 (Aug 30, PM) introduces an "Editorial Advisory" --- a
permanent, non-detachable evidence attachment shipped with every piece
of content --- and a Host Environment Colonization Guide keyed to THE
COLONIZATION MANUAL's own field-guide format. Both are strong patterns,
but both are scoped to REVERB's own warehoused-roster architecture (a
single card that contains other cards' functions, rather than
orchestrating separately seated cards the way the rest of the Media
Council works). Promoting either to a platform-wide domain would be a
materially larger design decision than this consolidation's mandate,
which is to unscatter features already intended as platform-wide, not to
platform-ize a specific card's architecture. Both are flagged here as
candidate proposals for a future ATLAS 360 PLAN revision, consistent
with how the AI Forensics Report's own recommendation became the
Linguistic Detectability Pass two amendment cycles later --- not adopted
silently, not adopted by default.

Carried Forward Unchanged

Domains 1--12 keep their v3.0 content and are not restated in full here
--- see the Domain Reference Table below and the v3.0 Complete Rewrite
document itself for their atomic prompts. Domain 13's original two
prompts (KLARITY-001, KLARITY-002, SKRIBE-001--003) are unchanged;
KLARITY-003 is additive.

Domain 14 --- SPC Cheat Sheet & Marketplace Discovery Standard (NEW)

**CHEAT-001 🔴 P0 --- SPC Cheat Sheet --- Platform-Wide Standard
Section**

*SPHINX ENGINE × ATLAS 360 PLAN · Deps: INTK2-002, SKRIBE-001*

ROLE: Marketplace-discovery engineer generalizing a per-card pattern
into a platform publication requirement.

INSTRUCTION: Every SPC card in the platform catalogue --- internal or
SPHINX Marketplace-listed --- now carries a standard Cheat Sheet section
at publication: a General Definition (mission, skill set, use cases,
third-party framing), a General Production Process (numbered usage
steps), and Environment-Specific Usage Notes across the ecosystem's ten
named development environments (Claude Skills, Claude Code,
Codex+Sites+GPT, Cursor, Replit, Antigravity, Google Gemini Studio,
Microsoft Azure Co-Pilot Studio, LangChain, GitHub Co-Pilot).

CONSTRAINT: C-117: this generalizes REVERB ALPHA v2.0 §7's card-level
pattern into a platform requirement --- not a new capability invented
for this build, an existing pattern promoted to a domain.

FORMAT: New spc_library.cheat_sheet_published boolean + linked
cheat_sheet document per card.

DATA: REVERB ALPHA v2.0 §7 and REVERB v3.0 §6 both already conform to
this shape; they are the first two compliant instances, not the origin
of a new requirement imposed on them retroactively.

**CHEAT-002 🟡 P1 --- SPHINX Ownership of Cheat Sheet Publishing &
Semantic Tagging**

*SPHINX ENGINE · Deps: CHEAT-001*

ROLE: Catalogue engineer assigning a clear, single owner for a new
platform-wide publication step.

INSTRUCTION: Formalize SPHINX ENGINE --- already the established
marketplace/catalogue engine per Domain 3 and Domain 5 of the v2.0 base
PDD --- as sole owner of Cheat Sheet publishing and semantic tagging
platform-wide.

CONSTRAINT: C-118: no PDD-authoring or card-rewrite capability is
implied by this ownership. That boundary --- disclosed in the -007
"TITAN" Honesty Gate note and the REVERB ALPHA "SPHINX" Honesty Gate
note --- is unchanged by this domain.

FORMAT: cheat_sheet.published_by = 'sphinx_engine' on every card
record.

DATA: Directly extends SPHINX's real, already-established role rather
than assigning it a new one.

**CHEAT-003 🟢 P2 --- Third-Party Definition Population Rule**

*SPHINX ENGINE · Deps: CHEAT-001*

ROLE: Catalogue engineer preventing speculative marketplace claims.

INSTRUCTION: A card's Cheat Sheet "Third-Party Definitions"
subsection stays empty until that card's actual first Marketplace
listing.

CONSTRAINT: C-119: matches REVERB ALPHA's own disclosed practice for
this exact subsection ("None exist\... populated at first Marketplace
listing, not before") --- codified platform-wide rather than left to
each card author's discretion.

FORMAT: cheat_sheet.third_party_definitions defaults to null, not an
empty-but-present placeholder.

**CHEAT-004 🟡 P1 --- Environment Sketches Are Sketches, Not Integration
Claims**

*SPHINX ENGINE × ATLAS 360 PLAN · Deps: CHEAT-001*

ROLE: Governance engineer preventing a documentation format from
drifting into an unverified capability claim.

INSTRUCTION: Every environment-specific usage note is explicitly labeled
a sketch of how a card's phase or prompt contracts would be implemented
if built in that environment --- never a claim that the integration
exists today, unless it verifiably does.

CONSTRAINT: C-120: directly inherits the disclosure pattern REVERB ALPHA
used for its own ten environment sketches ("a sketch\... not a
description of an existing integration. None of these exist today.")
--- that same disclaimer is now a platform requirement, not one card's
editorial choice.

FORMAT: cheat_sheet.environment_notes\[\].status enum:
{'sketch','verified_integration'}, defaulting to 'sketch'.

**CHEAT-005 🟢 P2 --- Retroactive Application to Existing Catalogue**

*ATLAS 360 PLAN × SPHINX ENGINE · Deps: CHEAT-001--004*

ROLE: Rollout engineer sequencing adoption without implying instant
non-compliance.

INSTRUCTION: Existing cards published before this domain shipped ---
REVERB, SHAYMXT, GRIOT SI, LUCI ULTRA SI, and the rest of the catalogue
--- are not required to retrofit a Cheat Sheet immediately.

CONSTRAINT: C-121: the requirement applies at next publication or
revision. This avoids implying the entire existing catalogue is out of
compliance the moment Domain 14 ships.

FORMAT: cheat_sheet_published defaults false on legacy records without
triggering a compliance flag; flips true at next card revision.
