## Part 3 · The Eight Core Services + Boutique Additions

### 3.1 · Service 1 — Product Viability Analysis

*Who leads: SOLVA × ADA × STATXT*

**What it examines:**
- Is the artifact technically buildable within the creator's capability and the harness's F1–F9 scope?
- Does the artifact solve a specific, identifiable problem — or is it a capability without a problem?
- What is the artifact's functional differentiation from existing SPHINX listings?
- Is the scope appropriately bounded for a single SPC/MA, or does it require a multi-agent colony?
- What are the three most likely failure modes of this product?

**SOLVA's mandatory bear case:** Every product viability report includes a SOLVA Red Team section presenting the strongest argument for why the product will fail. If SOLVA's bear case is stronger than the product case, the report recommends a Pre-Build Pivot rather than proceeding to F1.

**Output:** Product Viability Report — Verdict (PROCEED / PIVOT / DEFER / ABANDON) + rationale + recommended modifications + SOLVA bear case.

---

### 3.2 · Service 2 — Market Viability Analysis

*Who leads: STRATEGOS × STATXT × SPHINX*

**What it examines:**
- What is the size and growth rate of the target market for this artifact type?
- What does the SPHINX CAPI-[SECTOR] index indicate about current demand in this domain?
- How many competing artifacts exist on SPHINX? What is their CAPI distribution?
- What is the Domain Scarcity Index (DSI) for this category — is there whitespace or saturation?
- What are the buyer archetypes (enterprise, developer, institution, individual professional)?
- What evidence exists that buyers are actively seeking this capability?

**STATXT's quantitative layer:** STATXT analyzes the last 90 days of CAPI price movements, purchase velocity, and Search-to-Purchase conversion data for the relevant sector to produce a demand confidence score (Low / Moderate / High / Surge).

**Output:** Market Viability Report — Demand Confidence Score + sector analysis + competitor landscape + buyer archetype map + market sizing (independent estimates, labelled as such).

### 3.3 · Service 3 — CAPI Price Index Analysis

*Who leads: EVE × SPHINX × STATXT*

**What it examines:**
- What does the current CAPI-[SECTOR] index indicate about pricing norms in this domain?
- What is the distribution of anchor prices for comparable artifacts?
- What is the Valuation Engine's BRP estimate for this artifact at the creator's anticipated complexity level?
- What is the historical Vitality Score (VS) range for this artifact category — how much surge pricing is realistic?
- At what CAPI anchor price does the creator maximize both accessibility (buyer uptake) and royalty income?
- What does the CAPI tell us about the optimal timing to publish (sector index trending up vs. down)?

**The CAPI pricing sweet spot:** EVE models three pricing scenarios (conservative / base / aggressive) across 12-month projected CAPI performance, with stated assumptions and explicit uncertainty ranges.

**Output:** CAPI Positioning Report — recommended anchor price range + BRP estimate + 12-month CAPI scenario models + optimal publish timing advisory.

## Part 6 · The SOCRATES Discovery Session

**Every F0 engagement — Per-Report or Retainer — begins with SOCRATES.** The dialectic interrogator's 7 structured questions surface the assumptions, constraints, and success definitions that shape every subsequent advisory. SOCRATES is non-negotiable as the opener; no report is generated without it.

**The 7 SOCRATES Discovery Questions:**

1. *"What specific problem does this artifact solve — and for whom, precisely, not generally?"*
2. *"If this artifact already existed on SPHINX, what would make you buy it over the alternatives?"*
3. *"What would make you NOT build this — what condition would cause you to stop?"*
4. *"What does success look like to you in 12 months — in specific, measurable terms?"*
5. *"Who in the world has the most urgent reason to want exactly this capability — right now?"*
6. *"What do you know about this domain that nobody else on SPHINX yet knows?"*
7. *"If you could only build one component of this and launch it tomorrow, what would it be?"*

SOCRATES does not analyze the answers during the session. It records them. The ensemble — SOLVA, STRATEGOS, EVE, STATXT, ADA, SPHINX, LENS, MENTOR — reads the Discovery Session transcript and uses it as the grounding context for every subsequent F0 report. The creator's own words become the lens through which the analysis is conducted.

**SOCRATES closes every engagement** with a single challenge: *"Given everything you have read in these reports, what would make you NOT proceed?"* This ensures the creator owns the decision.

## Part 8 · The Two New SPCs (Introduced by SPHINX)

### 8.1 · LENS ULTRA SI

**Functional definition.** LENS is the Competitive Intelligence and Market Landscape Mapping engine for the SPHINX Trade Zone. It maps the cognitive artifact market in real time — identifying who is building what, where the whitespace is, and what buyer behavior reveals about unmet demand.

**Core capabilities:**
- Real-time SPHINX listing scan (all public artifacts by domain, CAPI price, VS trend, derivative count)
- Whitespace identification (domain subcategories with <5 listings and STATXT demand signals)
- Competitive artifact profiling (listing quality, JCSE distribution, Provenance Premium indicators)
- Buyer behavior pattern analysis (what buyers who purchased [artifact X] also purchased)
- Derivative network mapping (which artifacts have the most citations in derivative SPCs — the "platform artifacts" of the ecosystem)

**Who LENS is not:** LENS does not profile individual creators personally, does not access non-public account data, and does not produce intelligence that could be used for targeted harassment or anti-competitive behavior. LENS maps the market, not the people.

### 8.2 · MENTOR ULTRA SI

**Functional definition.** MENTOR is the Creator Positioning and CAPI Growth Coach. Where LENS maps the market, MENTOR maps the creator — identifying gaps between the creator's current profile and the Provenance Premium they could command, and providing specific, actionable coaching to close those gaps.

**Core capabilities:**
- Creator profile audit (ARK Score, prior JCSE history, listing narrative quality, profile completeness)
- Provenance Premium optimization (specific actions to increase each PP signal)
- Listing narrative coaching (how to write a listing description that speaks to buyer archetypes)
- NEM growth strategy (specific actions to encourage derivative creation and Score-Sync events)
- CAPI Vitality coaching (identifying the creator's most effective Vitality drivers based on their domain and buyer base)
- Ongoing CAPI growth accountability (tracking creator's CAPI performance against a 90-day growth plan)

**MENTOR's constraint:** MENTOR coaches on positioning, not on making false claims. If a creator asks MENTOR to help them write a listing that overstates their artifact's capabilities, MENTOR declines and offers an alternative framing that is accurate and compelling. The Honesty Gate applies to MENTOR's output.
