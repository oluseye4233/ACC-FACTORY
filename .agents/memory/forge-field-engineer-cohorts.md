FORGE FIELD ENGINEER is the requested subscription name for free, invite-only Intelligent Systems Architect and Engineer trainee cohorts. The Guide and Ascension Protocol provide the curriculum, with a worked example at each ARK Harness gate and a path toward existing Quest badges and certifications.

**Why:** The user defined this training offer and explicitly required the PDD to conform to existing Quest rules.

**How to apply:** Keep admission and course assessment separate from Quest eligibility. Do not add instructor approval, cohort completion, originality assessments or a new prerequisite badge chain to existing Quest rules. Do not weaken those rules for free students or grant credentials merely for joining. Individual course progress can differ from authoritative Quest status. The design document does not itself activate the service.

Individual students use a parallel verified identity, not the shared staff login. Accepted trainee invitations also confer a free-for-life Practitioner entitlement; course-funded educational capabilities remain separate from that entitlement.

**Why:** The shared staff entrance grants administrator access. On 2026-10-24 the user explicitly extended FORGE to give all invited course trainees free Practitioner for life. This is not permission to grant administration, Architect privileges or unlimited inference.

**How to apply:** Activate the benefit on verified invitation acceptance, including historical accepted trainee invitations, with no completion requirement, charge, card or renewal. Preserve it after course expiry, completion, suspension or membership revocation; account security still applies. Resolve at least Practitioner without downgrading higher plans or overwriting payment records. Existing paid subscriptions are separate and must not be silently cancelled or refunded. Recheck owned active membership and cohort state before reserving funded course inference; retain separate spent/reserved totals and existing company limits. Expired training access must not become a new prerequisite for ordinary earned Quest claims.

Accepted trainee invitations are the permanent grant evidence, not disposable enrollment metadata.

**Why:** A lifetime benefit must survive administrative course changes and payment-provider cancellations without a new purchase or manual backfill.

**How to apply:** Do not purge accepted invitation history as routine cleanup. If invitation retention ever changes, preserve or migrate permanent grants before deleting that evidence. Never infer a grant from an email match, client flag, unaccepted invitation or course attendance alone.

The student identity provider must persist across public entry, sign-in and authenticated routing, rather than mounting only after the existing server-cookie gate recognizes a student.

**Why:** Managed sign-in tickets can arrive before the API session cookie exists. Conditional mounting lost the browser bootstrap while token-authenticated backend requests appeared healthy; persistent provider wiring plus the cookie bridge passed the real student journey.

**How to apply:** Preserve SDK loading before student redirects, wildcard callback routes and cookie-only browser transport. Do not replace the staff entrance or add a Bearer-token workaround.

Shared registration and invitation links must resolve for existing staff sessions as well as signed-out invitees and signed-in students; preserve invitation tokens across authenticated entry redirects.

**Why:** The owner may open a QR link while already signed in as staff. Signed-out registration can render successfully while the authenticated route table falls through to 404, so an anonymous HTTP 200 or screenshot alone does not validate a shared enrollment link.

**How to apply:** Check all three session states when changing public enrollment URLs. Redirect existing identities to Training without changing their roles, and retain invitation context without allowing arbitrary external redirects.

Unfinalised programme reservations remain held until metered settlement or confirmed failure, even after their timeout.

**Why:** A dead worker or expired reservation is not evidence of zero provider charge. Automatically restoring a lifetime allowance on timeout could repeatedly fund unrecorded calls.

**How to apply:** Keep these holds separate from completed spend and require operator reconciliation for uncertain calls; do not silently recycle them as fresh student funding.

Convener management is cohort-specific: classes and invitations, trainee performance
feedback and personalized follow-up, cohort communications and meetings, and a
separate Exemplar collection for each class.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these responsibilities for FORGE FIELD
ENGINEER conveners, not a global trainee administration or public marketplace role.

**How to apply:** Keep learner feedback private to the learner and assigned
teaching staff. Sharing a training reference must not activate Sphinx publication,
and coaching recommendations must not substitute for existing Quest authority.
