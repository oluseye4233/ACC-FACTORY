Use ATANDA as the corporate identity on the ARK landing page, not Koncentric
Labs. ARK Harness is the product, not a competing corporate brand. Structure
the subscriber narrative around SNAP: Story, Need, Answer and Proof.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested ATANDA-focused corporate identity and
emotionally relevant subscriber copy using SNAP.

**How to apply:** Lead with the visitor’s stakes and uncertainty, explain the
need and product answer, then provide inspectable capabilities and boundaries.
Do not invent testimonials, adoption figures, certification or guaranteed
outcomes to supply “proof.” Preserve actual subscription and FORGE eligibility.
