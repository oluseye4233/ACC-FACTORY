name: IMPRIMA Portal publication scope
description: User-defined centralized publication, subscriber attribution, document identity and notification requirements.
---

IMPRIMA PORTAL is the requested Category A document-publication service for
ARK. The user wants existing upload/download controls replaced by portal
functions, with the IMPRIMA SPC team managing artifact publication.

Every download must require a single-use emailed publication code linked to
the subscriber profile. Uploads move into the central portal rather than
remaining distributed among the F-stage pages. Published documents must use professional, consistent KLARITY and
SKRIBE layout standards, clearly identify author and publisher, and carry
a date/time stamp with ATANDA.IMPRIMA at bottom right. A designated control
email address must automatically receive a copy of each downloaded artifact.

**Why:** The user is rescoping IMPRIMA around traceable publication and the
commercial objective of treating published artifacts as intellectual-property
assets, not merely providing another document-export button.

The user explicitly selected single-use emailed codes and centralized intake,
not reusable subscriber codes or merely rebranded per-stage upload controls.

**How to apply:** Centralize the actual server download paths as well as UI
controls; cosmetic button renaming is insufficient. Distinguish downloader,
author and publisher, preserve ownership and protected workflow boundaries,
and do not describe publication stamps as proof of copyright ownership,
registration, independent certification or a release approval.
